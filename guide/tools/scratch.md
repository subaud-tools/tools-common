## When to reach for it

- Something you'll need in ten minutes: a link, an error message, the next thing to try.
- A reminder your agent should leave for itself or for you, in the same session.

## When to skip it

- Anything you want to keep or share: put it in the repo, or in a document you edit in galley.
- Secrets: creel keeps those out of notes and chat.

## Where it sits

Beside the terminal, in each session's sidebar. Each session has its own pad, so notes from one piece of work don't spill into another.

## Pointers

- **It saves as you type.** There's no save key, and nothing is lost when you close it.
- **Your agent writes to the same pad.** What it adds appears in the open pad as you watch.
- **It never lands in your repo,** so it can't be committed by accident.
- **One pad per session:** two sessions on the same repo get two pads.
